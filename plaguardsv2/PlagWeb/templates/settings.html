Geolocation Databases

All optional; a blank field is skipped. Point these at MaxMind's local GeoLite2 databases and lookups never leave this machine. Fill in any mix of the three and they're merged into one record per address.

  1. Create a free account at MaxMind's GeoLite2 signup page.
  2. Once signed in, open Manage License Keys under your account and generate one. MaxMind requires it before any database can be downloaded.
  3. Go to Download Files and get GeoLite2-City, GeoLite2-Country and GeoLite2-ASN. The .tar.gz works as it is; there's no need to extract it.
  4. Paste each file's path, or the folder holding it, into the matching field.
