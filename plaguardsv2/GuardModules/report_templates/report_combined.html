Disclaimer & scope

{# A formal notice rather than loose prose: a bordered instrument with a banner, numbered clauses and an acknowledgement line, laid over the watermark that every page carries. #}
IMPORTANT — READ BEFORE RELYING ON THIS DOCUMENT
1. This report is an investigative aid, not a verdict. It records what static analysis could determine about each submitted input.
2. Nothing was executed. Deobfuscation is performed by rewriting literal values only. No part of the input was run, and no network connection was made on its behalf.
3. Deobfuscation is best-effort. Techniques that depend on runtime state cannot be resolved statically. The transform log records exactly what was applied; anything not listed there was left untouched.
4. Threat-intelligence results reflect third-party data at the time of the lookup and may change without notice. Indicators that are reserved or non-routable are marked as such rather than being reported as clean.
5. Indicator values are defanged (1[.]2[.]3[.]4, hxxp://) so that they cannot be clicked or resolved by accident. Refang them before use in any tooling.
6. The analyst verdict is authoritative. Severity reflects the technique observed, not confirmed impact. The reviewer's decision on each finding is what this report treats as its conclusion.
